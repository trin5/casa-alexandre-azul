Welcome

Welcome to Casa Alexandre Azul, a relaxed coastal apartment in the heart of Quarteira, just a short walk from Praia de Quarteira.

Recently renovated, the apartment is designed for comfortable, longer stays. With two bedrooms and two bathrooms, it accommodates up to four guests and offers a bright, open living space, a private balcony with a glimpse of the ocean, and everything needed to settle in with ease.

Air conditioning, fast WiFi, a fully equipped kitchen, and in-apartment laundry are all in place. Whether you're here to unwind, work remotely, or a bit of both.

The Location

Casa Alexandre Azul sits between the laid-back rhythm of Quarteira and the energy of Vilamoura Marina, just a 15-minute walk along the coastline. The beach is only 5 minutes away, and just outside your door you'll find a variety of local cafes, restaurants, and bakeries. Everything you need within easy walking distance.

A Simple Ritual

In the late afternoon, take a walk along the promenade by Praia de Quarteira as the light softens and the day begins to slow. Stop for a coffee, a cold beer, or a glass of wine by the sea, and follow the coastline toward Vilamoura Marina. It's a simple routine but one that quickly becomes part of daily life here.

We hope you enjoy the slower pace, the sea air, and your time at Casa Alexandre Azul.

Local Favourites

Morning

Start your day slowly with fresh bread and coffee from O Pao do Povo, just steps from the apartment. For something a little different, visit Boba Cha Cafe, a relaxed local spot run by Canadians and a personal favourite.

Lunch and Casual Dining

  • Tico Tico - classic seafood right on the beach
  • Taberna do Peixe - simple, authentic, and well-loved
  • Ca da Terra - relaxed atmosphere with great food

Afternoon and Sunset

Walk the promenade along Praia de Quarteira toward Vilamoura Marina as the afternoon light softens. Along the way, you'll find plenty of small cafes and casual spots to stop for a drink by the sea, or simply enjoy the walk and see where it takes you.

Living Here

Quarteira has a slower, more local rhythm than nearby Vilamoura Marina. It's the kind of place where routines come naturally: morning coffee, time by the water, and evenings that unfold without much planning. One of the best parts of staying here is how easily you can move between the two at your own pace on foot.
